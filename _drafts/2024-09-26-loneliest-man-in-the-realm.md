> The man who stands at the greatest height is the loneliest man in the realm - Shogun TV Series

A supervisor once told me, 'It's lonely at the top!' when I became a manager at a new company. In my previous role, I rose through the ranks and already had bonds with my team. But coming in as a new leader was different. It felt harder to connect—and yes, it was lonely.

Luckily, another mentor reminded me that you can build both personal and professional relationships with your team. It’s a tricky balance, but it can be done.
