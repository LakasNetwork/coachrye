I rewatched Kung Fu Panda 4 last weekend, and it's amazing how such a light-hearted movie has so many lessons packed into it. It's equally amazing how stepping back and taking a fresh look at things gives us new perspectives and insights.

This quote, in particular, grabbed my attention because I am very familiar with the fear of change:

> ... it's just easier to hold on to the life you know than move on to the one you don't.

## Holding On

I've been guilty many times of trying to hold on and keep things as they are.

Looking back, there have been a couple of work opportunities that I declined, even though they aligned more with my passions. I've failed to delegate tasks and chose to micromanage, all under the guise of making sure things get done right when it was just me trying to hold on to the way things are. I've tried to keep friendships when, deep down, I knew it was time to let go.

## Letting Go

As we reflect on our stories of holding on, it's important to give ourselves grace.

Letting go is not easy.

We invest so much in building that career, in that friendship, in our current state. The emotional attachment is hard to peel away from.

There's also the fear of the unknown. It seems easier to stay with what you have now, even if you've outgrown it because it's familiar and comfortable.

## Moving On

When we're at these crossroads, it's important to step back and see the benefits of moving on and not be overwhelmed by what we'll let go of. The chance to learn new things, meet new people, and experience new beginnings is worth it.

How about you? What are the things in your life you know you need to let go of and move on from?

Maybe it's time to embrace change, step out of your comfort zone, and expand it.
