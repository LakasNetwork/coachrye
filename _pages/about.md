            <h4 class="serif fw-bold mb-0">Harness Your Strengths. Architect Your Success.</h4>

            <div class="fs-5 text-muted mb-4">
                <p>I help professionals harness their natural talents and turn them into strengths that work for them. Using CliftonStrengths® discovery combined with strategic inquiry, I work with clients to define success on their terms, leverage their unique strengths, and architect sustainable high performance.</p>                
                <p>I've spent over two decades at the intersection of systems and people. From architecting solutions at global firms like <strong>Accenture</strong> and <strong>Oracle</strong> to facilitating performance for industry leaders like <strong>Globe Telecom, Nestlé, and Jollibee Food Corporation</strong>, I've developed a systems mindset that now informs how I coach.</p>                
                <p>I'm still in the tech world developing products, but discovered my deeper passion is helping people architect their success. Whether you're navigating a career transition, seeking to optimize your leadership, or building something new, I bring strategic inquiry and systems thinking to help you move from good to great to awesome.</p>
            </div>            
            <h5 class="fw-bold mb-3">How My Strengths Shape My Coaching:</h5>
            <div class="fs-6 text-muted mb-4">
                <p><strong class="text-primary">Individualization</strong> means I see you as unique. I don't give cookie-cutter advice. Every engagement is tailored to who you are and what you need.</p>                
                <p><strong class="text-primary">Learner</strong> keeps me genuinely curious about your situation. I ask questions to understand, not to prescribe. Discovery energizes me, and that's what I bring to our work together.</p>                
                <p><strong class="text-primary">Arranger</strong> helps me see how all the pieces of your life and work fit together. I help you organize complexity and create coherent systems aligned with your strengths.</p>                
                <p><strong class="text-primary">Maximizer</strong> focuses on taking what's already working and making it excellent. I help you go from good to great by optimizing your natural talents, not fixing what's "broken."</p>                
                <p><strong class="text-primary">Relator</strong> drives me to build deep, authentic partnerships. I'm not interested in surface-level transactions. I invest in understanding you and your goals completely.</p>
