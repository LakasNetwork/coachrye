I might have mentioned this before, but I believe life is about the pursuit of balance—the mix that works for you.

A balance between:

- Excellence and rest
- Courage and vulnerability
- Caring love and tough love

It's not about a perfect (50/50) split but about integrating the different areas of our lives in a way that makes sense for us and the people we love.
