We often say time heals, but does it really? Maybe time just gives us more experiences that shift our perspective. Maybe _we_ change.

<img src="/assets/images/2025-03-03-house.jpg" alt="House MD" style="width:500px;display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"/>

If you want to heal and be better then _do something!_

Tweak what you do. Change how you do it. Surround yourself with different people. Move. Change your environment. Time won’t do it for you. **You have to make the change.**
