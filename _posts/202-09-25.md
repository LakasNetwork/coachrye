Do what you love OR Do what you have to do... to do what you love.

I shared this with a friend years ago, but it soon became my own mantra when we moved to 🇨🇦.

I had no idea what would happen, but I knew I had to mentally and emotionally prepare myself—and be ready to do whatever it takes to make it work. For anyone on a similar journey, good luck to us.
