The golf journey continues to exceed expectations! After those successful private lessons I mentioned, Alaine and I decided to take the plunge and join group clinics. I'm in the men's clinic, she's in the ladies' clinic, and we're both loving every minute of it.

We've completed 3 sessions so far with 2 more to go, and here's the best part: Alaine is really starting to enjoy the game! Watching her excitement grow with each swing has been incredible. There's something special about having a hobby you can enjoy together while also challenging yourself individually.
